About Skytop

A conducive home for young minds

Located at 1, Odusanya Olugbode Avenue, Selewu Igbogbo, Skytop College provides a learning environment where students are encouraged to develop academically and morally, guided by trained and dedicated teachers and equipped laboratory facilities.

Every pillar of school life — from the classroom to the chapel — is designed to raise learners who are both intellectually sound and grounded in Christian values.

Vision & Mission

What drives every decision we make

“

Pioneering education that enhances dissemination of effective and functional knowledge in an academic friendly environment.

Our Vision
  • — Educate all learners irrespective of gender, ethnic, religious, and economic backgrounds.
  • — Acquire intellectual skills for clear thinking and expression.
  • — Train students to be tolerant and respect other people's points of view.
  • — Encourage leadership qualities.
Aims & Objectives

What we set out to achieve

  • Provide excellent educational services to all races irrespective of gender, religion, tribe or social status.

  • Instill high moral standards through religious education.

  • Equip students with intellectual skills for clear, rational and logical thinking.

  • Encourage Physical and Health Education and sporting talents.

  • Provide effective guidance and counselling.
